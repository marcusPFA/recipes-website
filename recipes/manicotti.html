Manicotti


Image by Marco Verch under CC BY 2.0 DE.

Description

Tender noodles stuffed with creamy ricotta, mozzarella, and Parmesan for a satisfying dish. Very delicious.

  • 5 ½ ounces manicotti pasta
  • 1 pint part-skim ricotta cheese
  • 8 ounces shredded mozzarella cheese
  • ¾ cup grated Parmesan cheese, divided
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon dried parsley
  • salt and ground black pepper to taste
  • 1 (16 ounce) jar spaghetti sauce

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).

  2. Cook manicotti in boiling water until al dente, about 10 to 12 minutes; drain and rinse with cold water.

  3. Mix ricotta, mozzarella, 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese, eggs, parsley, salt, and pepper in a large bowl until well combined.

  4. Pour 1/2 cup spaghetti sauce into an 11x17-inch baking dish. Stuff each manicotti shell with 3 tablespoons cheese mixture, and arrange over sauce.
    Pour remaining sauce over the top of the shells and sprinkle with remaining Parmesan cheese.

  5. Bake in the preheated oven until bubbly, about 45 minutes.

  6. Serve and enjoy.
